## Background

A. The Company is a registered company and carries on business, including a software
   business line trading as "**Radbit Studios**" and delivering the "**Radbit Auto**"
   vehicle sales and import workspace (the "**Line**").

B. The Line processes personal data and must comply with the Cyber and Data Protection
   Act [Chapter 12:07] and SI 155 of 2024 (the "**DPA Laws**").

C. The Company wishes to authorise the Line and the compliance programme necessary to
   operate it lawfully.

## Resolutions

**1. Confirmation of the Line.** The Company confirms that Radbit Auto is a **business
line of the Company**, and that the Company is the legal entity that operates it and
holds the resulting data.

**2. Approval to operate.** The Company approves the operation, hosting and support of
the Line, including the processing of tenant-agency customer data as a **processor**
on the documented instructions of each tenant agency.

**3. Authority of the CTO/officer.** The Company authorises [NAME], in the role of
[CTO / Director], to act on the Company's behalf for the Line, including to:

   (a) register and/or license the Company with the **Data Protection Authority (POTRAZ)**
       and file any trans-border (s28/s29) notification or authorisation;
   (b) sign **data processing agreements** with tenant agencies in the Company's name;
   (c) **appoint and notify a Data Protection Officer** to POTRAZ, chosen so as to be
       **independent** of the Company's decision-makers;
   (d) engage counsel and a DPO, and incur reasonable related fees up to
       **[USD ______]** without further approval, and above that sum with [approval body];
   (e) move hosting/billing for the Line to a **Company-owned account**;
   (f) sign the **IP/ownership note** recording whether the Radbit Auto apparatus is a
       Company asset or is licensed to the Company.

**4. Indemnity.** The Company shall indemnify the officer for acts done in good faith
within the authority set out above, in accordance with the Company's constitution.

**5. Effective date.** This resolution takes effect on [DATE].
