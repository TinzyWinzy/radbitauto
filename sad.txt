System Architecture Document: Multi-Tenant Vehicle Import Customer Portal

1. Executive Architectural Overview

The Zimbabwean automotive sector is currently navigating a period of unprecedented expansion, with passenger vehicle imports rising from USD 2.5 million per month in 2023 to a projected USD 6 million by March 2026. This architecture provides the technical blueprint for a centralized "System of Record" designed to replace the current "System of Engagement"—characterized by fragmented WhatsApp threads and manual document handling—with a structured, data-driven import dashboard.

Technical Objective: To consolidate logistical, financial, and regulatory data into a single source of truth, thereby reducing the logistical latency inherent in manual cross-border operations.

Operational Pain Points Solved:

* Visibility Gap: Eliminates informational asymmetry regarding vehicle location and import status.
* Payment Fragmentation: Centralizes quotation management, multi-currency payment history, and outstanding balance tracking.
* Document Centralization: Provides a secure repository for essential certificates, mitigating the risk of lost physical documentation.
* Communication Inefficiency: Streamlines customer enquiries through deep-linked, structured notification protocols.
* Regulatory Compliance: Automates the validation of vehicle eligibility based on age and pre-shipment inspection requirements.

This architecture bridges the gap between the informal "shadow cash economy" and formal business systems, providing the technical market requirements necessary for scalability in the Zimbabwean automotive sector.

2. Market Context & System Rationale

The architecture must navigate specific local regulatory hurdles and economic conditions, including volatile trade deficits and strict import bans.

Market Realities

Based on ZimStat and OEC World data, the system is optimized for the following volume and sourcing trends:

Metric	Detail
Market Dominance	Used imports (>95%) vs. New vehicles (<2,700 units/year)
Primary Sourcing	South Africa (61M), Japan (50M), United Kingdom ($5.53M)
Import Volume Trends	Sustained growth to USD 6M/month by Q1 2026
Primary Transit Corridors	Durban (RSA), Beira (MOZ), and Walvis Bay (NAM)

Compliance Validation Layer

The system implements a mandatory "Logic Gate" layer to enforce Statutory Instruments (SI 54 of 2024 and SI 59 of 2026) and Public Notice 84 of 2024.

* Age-Restriction Logic: The system automatically flags cases for "Import License Required" if the manufacture_year exceeds 10 years for passenger cars, double-cabs, and vans.
* Exemption Handling: Logic gates allow overrides for heavy commercial haulage trucks, tractors, and "Antique/Classic" vehicles (25+ years) provided they meet historical preservation criteria.
* Pre-Shipment Inspection (PVoC): The architecture enforces the June 2022 mandate requiring EAA (East Africa Automobile Services) certificates for all Japanese imports before status can advance to "Shipped."

3. Multi-Tenant Logical Architecture

To support diverse clients such as Eddy Customs and Muchy Motors on a single codebase, we employ a multi-tenant isolation strategy that ensures strict data privacy and branding autonomy.

Tenant Configuration Model

Administrators can configure unique tenant profiles including:

* Branding: Logo, custom Case ID Prefix (e.g., EC- vs MM-), and UI color palettes.
* Local Contact: Dedicated WhatsApp numbers and branch physical addresses.
* Operational Logic: Toggleable import stages and specific payment instructions (EcoCash, InnBucks, Bank).

Absolute Isolation via Row Level Security (RLS)

Data integrity is maintained through Row Level Security (RLS) within the PostgreSQL schema. This is not merely an API-level filter; the database policy mandates that every query is strictly scoped to the company_id of the authenticated tenant. This ensures that a Muchy Motors operative cannot query Eddy Customs’ records even in the event of an application-layer compromise.

4. Frontend Architecture: Offline-First PWA

The frontend is a Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for a "Data-Light Architecture," catering to the high data costs and intermittent connectivity found along transit corridors like Beitbridge.

Technical PWA Specifications

* Service Workers: Implements "Offline Shell" caching to ensure the dashboard structure loads instantly regardless of network state.
* Web App Manifest: Facilitates Android-optimized home screen installation to bypass app store frictions.
* Latency-Agnostic UI: Background sync allows staff at border posts to update status offline, with automatic reconciliation once connectivity is restored.

5-Second Answer UX Rule

The visual hierarchy is built around operational utility rather than marketing. Upon login, a customer must be able to answer the following within 5 seconds:

1. Status: High-contrast indicator of the current import milestone.
2. Financial Position: Clear "Total Due" vs. "Paid" balance.
3. Document Availability: Instant access to the Bill of Lading or EAA certificate.

5. Import Workflow & State Machine Design

The system treats the 24–34 day shipping window as a formalized state machine to eliminate operational ambiguity.

14-Stage Workflow with Transition Guards

The following sequence captures metadata including Status, Date Completed, and Staff Notes. Transition Guards are enforced to prevent "milestone jumping."

1. Enquiry
2. Quotation
3. Payment Confirmed
4. Vehicle Sourced
5. Purchase Completed (Guard: Requires Purchase Invoice upload)
6. Export Processing (Guard: Requires EAA Certificate upload for Japan sourcing)
7. Shipped (Guard: Requires Bill of Lading metadata)
8. In Transit
9. Arrived
10. Customs Clearance
11. Duties/Charges
12. Registration/Compliance
13. Ready for Collection
14. Delivered

6. Communication & WhatsApp Integration Layer

This architecture works with WhatsApp to leverage its dominance in the local cash economy without ceding control of the data audit trail.

Event-Driven Notification Architecture

The system utilizes an asynchronous event trigger for all status changes. When import_cases.status updates (e.g., to 'Arrived'), an event triggers the following:

* Notification Generator: Creates an in-app alert.
* Deep-Link Integration: Generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message link for the customer (e.g., "Hello, I need an update on my Hilux, Case EC-1042").
* Audit Logging: Records the notification event in the immutable system log.

7. Data Model & Multi-Tenant Schema

The relational PostgreSQL schema utilizes a normalized structure to support complex valuation and multi-tenancy.

Core Entities

* companies: Tenant master records.
* vehicles: Includes vin_chassis, engine_number, variant, source_country, and purchase_price.
* quotation_items: Supports the CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) valuation methodology, including freight invoices, insurance statements, and port charges.
* import_cases: The central junction linking vehicle, customer, and state.
* documents: Encrypted metadata for the vault.

8. Security, RBAC & Document Vault

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Role	Permissions
Customer	View-only access to own cases, financial status, and documents.
Staff	CRUD permissions for cases, payments, and document uploads. No tenant config.
Administrator	Full system access, staff management, branding, and stage configuration.

Document Vault & S3 Logic

Sensitive documents (Bill of Lading, EAA Certificates) are stored in encrypted S3/Object Storage.

* Access Control: The file path is obfuscated and never exposed publicly.
* Pre-signed URLs: Access is granted via temporary, expiring URLs generated only upon server-side authorization of the user’s company_id and role.

9. Operational Implementation & MVP Roadmap

Phase 1: Core Operations

* Authentication & Multi-tenant configuration.
* Basic Import Case management and 14-stage state machine.
* Customer dashboard (PWA Shell).

Phase 2: Finance & Documents

* CIF-based Quotation engine and manual payment recording.
* Secure Document Vault with S3 integration and Pre-signed URLs.

Phase 3: Advanced PWA & Tracking

* Full offline shell caching and background sync.
* Event-driven notification system.
* Manual coordinate tracking updates.

Phase 4: Future Integrations

* Fiscalisation Integration: Asynchronous communication with ZIMRA’s ASYCUDA system or local fiscal devices.
* WhatsApp Business API for automated outbound messaging.
* Payment gateway hooks for EcoCash/InnBucks.

Success Criteria

The architecture is successful if it facilitates answers to seven critical questions within 5 seconds of app launch:

1. What vehicle am I importing?
2. How much have I paid?
3. How much do I still owe?
4. Where is my vehicle currently located?
5. What is the next milestone?
6. Are my documents available for download?
7. How do I contact my agent?

By standardizing these data points, the system transforms the high-risk, opaque process of vehicle importation into a professional, scalable service.
