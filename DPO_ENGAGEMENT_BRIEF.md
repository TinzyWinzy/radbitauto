## 2. Why we need a DPO

- The **Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]** designates Potraz as the
  Data Protection Authority.
- The **Licensing of Data Controllers and Appointment of Data Protection Officers
  Regulations, 2024 (SI 155 of 2024)** requires licensing/registration of data
  controllers and appointment of a Data Protection Officer.
- Our data is hosted outside Zimbabwe, engaging the **trans-border transfer
  provisions (s28/s29)**.
- We need a DPO to guide registration, advise on lawful basis, consent, data-subject
  rights and cross-border transfer, and to act as the contact point with Potraz.

## 3. Scope of the DPO role (part-time)

1. Advise on and support **Potraz data-controller registration/licensing**.
2. Advise on the **controller vs processor** position of Radbit Studios relative to
   its tenant agencies, and the correct allocation of obligations between them.
3. Guide the **trans-border transfer** process (notification/authorisation, DPIA/DTIA,
   transfer terms, and consent where required).
4. Advise on **lawful basis, consent capture and records**, and privacy-notice content.
5. Advise on **data-subject rights** (access, correction, erasure, portability) and
   our handling process.
6. Advise on **breach detection, notification and record-keeping** obligations.
7. Advise on **security and data-minimisation** expectations proportionate to our risk.
8. Provide input into **vendor/processor agreements** (e.g. our data-processing terms
   with tenant agencies and cloud providers).
9. Maintain an **annual compliance review** note and a register of processing activities.

## 4. Deliverables

- Written guidance on the registration/licensing steps and evidence of filing support.
- A concise **DPIA/DTIA** for cross-border processing (or review of one we draft).
- A **consent capture + privacy notice** specification for our signup and customer
  intake screens.
- A **breach-notification runbook** (internal contact, Potraz contact, timelines).
- A **data-subject request** procedure (intake, verification, response template).
- Input into our **processor/sub-processor agreement** templates.
- A short **annual review** report and a processing register.
