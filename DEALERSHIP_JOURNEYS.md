# Connected dealership journeys

## Discover and enquire

The dealer publishes available stock in its own `/showroom/{slug}`. Each listing has photos, location, USD asking price, a WhatsApp enquiry link and a showroom enquiry form. Unpublished, reserved and sold stock is excluded. Supplier references are never presented as locally available stock.

## Convert interest

Dealer → Leads → Continue enquiry creates a customer from the lead or links an existing customer. Retrying conversion returns the same customer. Continue as Stock sale selects that customer in the reservation form; Continue as Customer import opens a prefilled import enquiry. Follow-up dates and salesperson assignment can be updated without losing the customer link.

## Retail buyer access

Dealer → Sales → Purchase details and documents opens the transaction. The owner can create a private buyer invitation using the existing verified-email invitation flow. A linked buyer sees purchases in My vehicles and opens their own purchase, terms, confirmed payments, balance, collection status and issued documents. Other buyers and other dealerships cannot access the sale.

## Sales documents

Staff issue a numbered sale agreement, balance statement, payment/refund receipt or completed handover record. Documents save immutable snapshots when issued. Print/save PDF renders the saved snapshot. Subsequent payments and corrections do not change prior documents. Buyers can view and print documents issued for their purchase.

## Stock photography

Dealer → Stock supports JPEG, PNG and WebP uploads from phone/file selection, up to eight images under 5 MB each. The server checks storage path, image signature and tenant ownership. Public image tokens are issued only for vehicle images; private import documents use separate protected paths. Staff can choose a cover, remove an image from the listing or retain supplier-provided HTTPS image references.

## Dealer acquisition

Owner → Buy and import dealer stock → select an unallocated registered vehicle → enter supplier link/reference. Record actual purchase and landing/preparation costs with evidence. Progress selected → purchased → shipped → arrived → cleared → prepared. Preparation transfers the vehicle into available stock with its accumulated costs, location and asking price. No customer record is created. Vehicle allocation and transfer are transactional; the vehicle cannot also become a customer import.

## Money earned

Retail sale margin uses agreed selling price minus acquisition/settlement and direct costs. Complete-cost delivered deals are distinguished from estimates. Owners can correct stock costs and available-stock prices with an audited reason.

Import payment entry distinguishes agency receipts from customer payments directly to suppliers, and vehicle/import charges from service fees or mixed amounts. Both confirmed recipients settle quotation obligations. Supplier-direct amounts are excluded from agency collections. Reports show each category separately. Owners separately record fees/commissions actually earned and expenses borne by the dealer; corrections create compensating entries and retain the originals. Recorded deal results exclude overheads and unrecorded expenses.

## Deliberate operating choices

Supplier purchases, payment transfers and WhatsApp sending remain manual. Staff confirm evidence, customer agreement and physical handover. Reservation expiry blocks further payments; staff refund/cancel reservations before rebooking. Customer accounts remain optional for dealer-assisted transactions. Digital signatures, automatic supplier buying and full accounting are outside these journeys.
