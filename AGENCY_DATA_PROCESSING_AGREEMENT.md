## 1. Definitions

Terms used in the CDPA have the same meaning here. In addition:

- **"Applicable Law"** — the CDPA [Chapter 12:07], SI 155 of 2024, and any guidelines
  or directions issued by POTRAZ (the Data Protection Authority).
- **"Personal Data"** — any information relating to an identifiable data subject,
  described in **Annex A**.
- **"Processing"** — any operation performed on Personal Data (collection, storage,
  retrieval, use, disclosure, erasure, destruction, etc.).
- **"Sub-processor"** — a third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal
  Data on the Controller's behalf (listed in **Annex C**).
- **"Security Breach"** — as defined in the CDPA and SI 155 of 2024.
- **"Trans-border flow"** — the transfer of Personal Data outside Zimbabwe.

## 2. Scope and purpose of processing

2.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data **only** to provide the Service and
**only on the documented instructions of the Controller**, including as set out in
Annex A and any configuration the Controller applies in the Service.

2.2 The Processor shall **not**:
(a) process Personal Data for its own purposes;
(b) reuse Personal Data for marketing, analytics, or any product outside the Service;
(c) determine the purposes or essential means of processing; or
(d) transfer Personal Data to any third party except as permitted in this DPA.

2.3 The Processor shall inform the Controller immediately if, in its opinion, an
instruction would breach Applicable Law.

## 3. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

3.1 **Process only on instruction** — comply with clause 2 and the CDPA duty that a
processor processes data only as instructed by the controller.

3.2 **Confidentiality** — ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data are
bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

3.3 **Security** — implement the technical and organisational measures in **Annex B**,
and assist the Controller in meeting the CDPA security (s18) obligations.

3.4 **Assist with data-subject rights** — assist the Controller, by appropriate
technical and organisational measures, in responding to data-subject requests (access,
correction, erasure, portability), using the Service's export and account features.

3.5 **Assist with impact assessments** — provide reasonable information to support any
data protection impact assessment or trans-border assessment the Controller carries out.

3.6 **Records** — keep a record of processing activities carried out on behalf of the
Controller, as required by Applicable Law.

3.7 **Breach notification** — notify the Controller **within 24 hours** of becoming
aware of a Security Breach, with the information in clause 8.

3.8 **Cooperation with POTRAZ** — cooperate with the Controller and POTRAZ in relation
to the exercise of their functions, and support the Controller's DPO.

## 4. Controller obligations

The Controller shall:

4.1 determine the lawful basis for processing and ensure it has the right to share the
Personal Data with the Processor;

4.2 provide privacy information to data subjects and obtain any consent required,
including for any trans-border flow (see clause 6);

4.3 ensure its instructions to the Processor comply with Applicable Law;

4.4 appoint and notify a DPO to POTRAZ **if required** (noting that a Tier-1
small-scale controller, or a party not acting as a controller, may be exempt), and
maintain its own registration/licensing position with POTRAZ where required;

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Controller **authorises** the Sub-processors listed in **Annex C** (principally
**Google Firebase / Google Cloud**).

5.2 The Processor shall give the Controller reasonable notice of any intended change to
Sub-processors, and shall impose data-protection obligations on each Sub-processor
**no less protective** than this DPA.

5.3 The Processor remains responsible for its Sub-processors' performance of their
obligations.

## 6. Trans-border flow

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that the Service hosts Personal Data **outside Zimbabwe**
(currently Google Firebase, United States) (see Annex C).

6.2 The **Controller is responsible** for compliance with the CDPA trans-border
provisions (s28/s29), including notification/authorisation to POTRAZ, adequacy or other
lawful basis, and obtaining any required explicit consent.

6.3 The Processor shall provide the information reasonably needed by the Controller for
that purpose, and shall not transfer Personal Data to a new jurisdiction without the
Controller's instruction.

6.4 The Processor shall enter into the transfer terms the Controller reasonably
requires, and shall give effect to any lawful instruction to delete or return Personal
Data.

## 7. Data-subject requests

7.1 The Processor shall not respond to a data-subject request directly unless
authorised by the Controller or required by law, and shall promptly refer such requests
to the Controller.

7.2 The Processor shall provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to
respond within the timelines in Applicable Law.

## 8. Security breach notification

8.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller **within 24 hours** of becoming aware of
a Security Breach, providing:
(a) the nature of the breach and categories/number of data subjects affected;
(b) likely consequences; and
(c) measures taken or proposed.

8.2 The Processor shall cooperate with the Controller's notification to POTRAZ and
affected data subjects, and shall not make public statements about the breach without
the Controller's agreement, except where required by law.

## 9. Audit and information

9.1 The Processor shall make available to the Controller the information reasonably
necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and shall allow audits by the
Controller or its appointed auditor, subject to confidentiality and reasonable notice
and not more than once per year except after a breach.

## 10. Return and deletion

10.1 On termination the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, return or delete
Personal Data within [30] days, except where retention is required by law.

10.2 The Controller acknowledges that the Service retains **recoverable backup copies**
(daily backups 28 days; point-in-time recovery 7 days), and that deletion from live
records does not immediately remove backups.
