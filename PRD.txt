Product Requirement Document: Multi-Tenant Vehicle Import Customer Portal (MVP)

1. Executive Summary & Core Product Vision

The strategic objective of this portal is to modernize the Zimbabwean vehicle importation lifecycle by transitioning stakeholders from fragmented, high-friction communication—primarily unmanaged WhatsApp threads and scattered emails—to a centralized, operational dashboard. In a high-value, cash-dominated economy where vehicle imports accounted for approximately $59.4 million in April 2025 (7.6% of the national import bill), providing a single source of truth is the primary mechanism for building trust.

The portal solves critical "information gaps" identified in the sourcing and clearing process: status uncertainty, document fragmentation, and opaque financial obligations. This is not a marketing site; it is an operational "Professional Operating Platform."

Strategic Targets:

* Tenants: Initial deployment focuses on white-labeled solutions for Eddy Customs and Muchy Motors, with the capacity to scale to any sourcing business or clearing agent.
* Vision: To provide a branded, professional interface that handles the complexities of the Zimbabwean regulatory landscape while maintaining the speed of local communication habits.

This vision is underpinned by a robust technical architecture designed to support specific user personas and mandatory compliance gates.

2. User Personas & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

A granular RBAC model is critical for maintaining data integrity and tenant isolation. In a multi-tenant environment, the system must ensure that data payloads and document access are strictly restricted to the relevant company_id to prevent cross-tenant data leakage.

Role	Primary Responsibilities	Key Permissions
Customer	Monitor import progress; manage payments and documentation.	View active imports/quotations; download documents; submit enquiries; WhatsApp deep-link access.
Staff	Manage the operational lifecycle of vehicle imports for specific customers.	Create/edit customers/cases; upload documents; manual tracking updates; record cash/bank payments.
Administrator	Oversee business operations, branding, and system configuration.	All Staff permissions plus: Manage staff accounts; configure custom workflow stages; define company-wide settings.

Customer Home Screen Requirements

The primary interface is a mobile-first "My Imports" dashboard. Each vehicle import is represented as a card-based interface showing the vehicle make/model (e.g., Toyota Hilux 2019), status (e.g., 🚢 In Transit), and a progress indicator. The "View Import" primary action serves as the gateway to detailed tracking and financial data.

This model ensures that users only interact with the specific regulatory and financial data pertinent to their role.

3. Regulatory & Compliance Context (Zimbabwe)

The Zimbabwean vehicle import landscape is defined by high volatility in statutory requirements. Automated compliance checks within the portal are strategically vital to prevent the importation of prohibited assets.

Vehicle Age Restrictions

Based on SI 54 of 2024 and SI 59 of 2026, the system must enforce the following logic:

* Prohibited (General): Passenger cars, passenger vans (carrying 10+ people), and double-cab trucks older than 10 years from the date of manufacture.
* Exempt (Heavy Commercial): Only heavy commercial vehicles (haulage trucks, tractors, specialized mining/construction equipment) remain exempt from the 10-year age cap.

Special Exemptions (SI 172 of 2024)

The system must support specific exemption flags for:

* Deceased Estates: Inherited vehicles.
* Returning Residents (Immigrant’s Rebate): Requires proof of 2+ years abroad (or 6 months for government assignment) and 6+ months of vehicle ownership.
* Antique/Classic Vehicles: Vehicles 25+ years old (requires an Import Licence from the Ministry of Industry and Commerce).

Mandatory Inspection

The portal serves as a "hard gate" for the EAA (East Africa Automobile Services) PVoC program. No import case can progress to the "Customs Clearance" stage without a verified EAA Certificate of Conformity.

These regulatory constraints directly inform the functional logic of the duty calculation and workflow engines.

4. Functional Requirements: The Import Workflow & Engines

The 14-stage workflow is the backbone of the platform, transforming a complex logistics process into a transparent timeline for the end customer.

The 14-Stage Import Workflow

The default sequence is: (1) Enquiry, (2) Quotation, (3) Payment Confirmed, (4) Vehicle Sourced, (5) Purchase Completed, (6) Export Processing, (7) Shipped, (8) In Transit, (9) Arrived, (10) Customs Clearance, (11) Duties/Charges, (12) Registration/Compliance, (13) Ready for Collection, (14) Delivered.

* Architectural Note: Administrators must have the ability to enable/disable, rename, and reorder these stages per tenant (e.g., a sourcing business vs. a clearing agent).

CIF Duty Calculation Engine

The engine must calculate duties based on the Cost, Insurance, and Freight (CIF) value. To prevent under-valuation penalties, the engine must use the Japanese Yellow Book Value as the base if it exceeds the purchase invoice price.

* Customs Duty: 25% to 40% (engine size dependent).
* Import Duty: Standard surcharge of ~20%.
* VAT: 15% (applied to CIF + Customs Duty + Import Duty).
* Surtax: 25% for vehicles >5 years old.
* Carbon Tax: Capacity-based flat fee.

Document Vault & WhatsApp Optimization

The "Document Vault" manages mandatory categories (Registration Certificate, Export Certificate, Bill of Lading, EAA Certificate). To harmonize with local behavior, the portal utilizes WhatsApp Bundle Optimization. High-friction screens (Quotation and Payment) must include deep-linked "WhatsApp Us" buttons that pre-fill the company number with the reference (e.g., "Update on EC-1042"), ensuring the system works with, not against, current communication habits.

5. Technical Architecture & Database Design

The platform follows a "Multi-Tenant Isolation" model. Every API request and database query must include a company_id filter at the schema level to ensure strict client-side and server-side authorization.

Relational Schema (Core Entities)

To support a production-ready environment, the relational data model includes:

* companies & company_settings (Branding, stage ordering, currency).
* users, customers, & staff (RBAC data).
* import_cases & vehicles (Chassis/VIN, year, make).
* import_stages & import_stage_updates.
* quotations & quotation_items (Individual line items for CIF).
* payments, documents, tracking_updates, notifications, & enquiries.

PWA Requirements

The PWA must be optimized for the Zimbabwean Android-dominated mobile market:

* Manifest & Service Workers: For an installable, app-like experience with an offline shell.
* Field Photography: The architecture must allow the PWA to access the camera to capture and upload photos of physical cash receipts or bank Proof of Payments (PoP) in the field, a critical requirement for the shadow cash economy.

6. Implementation Roadmap & MVP Success Criteria

Deployment is phased to transition tenants from manual tracking to integrated automation.

Phased Development Table

Phase	Focus	Key Deliverables
Phase 1	Infrastructure	Multi-tenant isolation, core RBAC, and Basic Import Case/Vehicle profiles.
Phase 2	Financials & Docs	CIF Engine (Yellow Book Logic), Document Vault, and WhatsApp Deep Links.
Phase 3	Experience	Tracking updates, PWA offline shell, and field photo capture for receipts.
Phase 4	Integration	Future APIs for payment gateways and automated shipping tracking.

MVP Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if a customer can answer these questions within 5 seconds of opening the app:

1. How much do I owe? (Outstanding balance via quotation engine).
2. Where is my vehicle? (Current tracking location).
3. What stage is it at? (Visual workflow timeline).
4. How do I contact the company? (Contextual WhatsApp deep-link).

Market Opportunity

Data from ZimStat and IH Securities indicates passenger vehicle imports grew from $2.5M/month in 2023 to $6M/month by March 2026. With used imports exceeding 95% of total acquisitions, this portal functions as the essential professional operating platform for a high-growth sector.
